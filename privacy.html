Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 May 2026. This draft should be reviewed by a qualified UK/Scottish solicitor before public launch.

1. Who we are

PropertyPanel is a property finance and portfolio management service for UK property investors and landlords. For now, the data controller is: [insert legal business name], [insert registered address], [insert contact email].

2. Personal data we collect

We may collect account details, contact details, subscription and billing references, property portfolio information, mortgage details, rent and cashflow data, tenant and guarantor contact details you choose to store, landlord details for managed or rent-to-rent records, reminder preferences, transaction records, uploaded document metadata and support messages.

If document uploads are enabled, users may upload tenancy agreements, mortgage offers, safety certificates, insurance documents, invoices and related property records. Users are responsible for ensuring they have a lawful basis to upload any third-party personal data, including tenant, guarantor, landlord or supplier data.

3. How we use personal data

We use personal data to provide the PropertyPanel service, create and manage accounts, store property portfolios, calculate estimates, organise documents, record income and expenses, generate user-requested reports, send selected reminders, process subscriptions, provide support, improve product security and comply with legal obligations.

4. Lawful basis

Under UK GDPR, we rely on different lawful bases depending on the activity:

  • Contract: to provide accounts, paid subscriptions, portfolio tools, document storage and reminders.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the service secure, prevent misuse, improve the product and respond to support requests.
  • Consent: for optional marketing emails or optional cookies where required.
  • Legal obligation: where we must keep payment, accounting or compliance records.

5. Payments

Payments are processed by Stripe. PropertyPanel does not store full card details. Stripe may process payment information according to its own privacy terms.

6. Service providers

We may use trusted providers for hosting, authentication, database storage, private file storage, payment processing, analytics, email reminders and customer support. Current intended providers include Supabase, Stripe, Vercel and Resend.

7. International transfers

Some providers may process data outside the UK. Where this happens, we will rely on appropriate safeguards such as UK-approved transfer mechanisms or equivalent contractual protections.

8. Data retention

We keep account and portfolio data while the account is active. Users can export a copy of their portfolio data from the Account area. Users can also delete portfolio data, including saved properties, documents and transaction records. Billing, subscription and payment records may be retained where needed for legal, accounting, security or dispute-resolution reasons.

If an account is closed, we will delete or anonymise personal data within a reasonable period unless we need to keep it for those legal, accounting, security or dispute-resolution reasons.

9. Your rights

10. Security

We use technical and organisational measures designed to protect personal data, including account authentication, access controls, row-level database security rules and private file storage controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Cookies and analytics

PropertyPanel may use essential cookies for authentication and security. Optional analytics or marketing cookies will be used only where legally permitted and, where required, with consent.

12. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy as PropertyPanel develops. Material changes will be communicated through the service or by email where appropriate.
